# Swapcast - 90-second finals pitch

Finals, Nov 5. Judges: Jason Calacanis, Cedric the Entertainer, Alex Elias.
Read times are targets. Practice once out loud; it should land at ~90s.

---

## The script

**Problem (0:00-0:15)**
"The median podcast episode gets 27 downloads in its first week. The single best free way to grow is a promo swap - two shows promote each other. But most swaps fail, because people pick the wrong partner."

**Insight (0:15-0:30)**
"A good partner is three things at once: your size, in an adjacent lane so you don't cannibalize each other, and their audience likes you back - not just you liking them. A plain language model can't check any of that. It just name-drops the biggest show in your genre. Only a taste graph can. That's Qloo."

**Live demo (0:30-1:10)** - screen share, show already picked
"Here's Swapcast. I pick my show - and I confirm the match, the agent never guesses for me. Watch the trace: it pulls my listeners' tastes, finds shows in my size band, then runs each candidate in reverse to check the fit both ways.
[point at a two-way match] This one: Qloo ranks them number 2 for my listeners, and ranks me number 4 for theirs. That's a real mutual fit.
[open the pitch] It drafts the outreach with that evidence baked in, plus a tracking link so we both measure the lift. And over here - brands my listeners already favor, as sponsor leads."

**Business + close (1:10-1:30)**
"Jason, you run podcasts, so you know this pain. The buyers are the two-plus million podcasters and the hosting platforms that want to keep them growing. Freemium for creators, a Pro tier for tracking and a partner CRM, and a white-label 'Grow' tab for hosting platforms. US podcast ads were 2.9 billion dollars last year. Swapcast turns the taste graph into the one thing a small creator can't get anywhere else: the right partner, and the pitch to win them. Thank you."

---

## Delivery notes

- **Pre-load the demo.** Have the show already typed and the result cached (run it once right before) so it appears in seconds. Never wait on a cold API call on stage.
- **Point, don't read.** On the two-way match, physically point at the two rank numbers. That's the "aha".
- **Separate Qloo from you.** Say "Qloo says #2" vs "Swapcast suggests a promo swap." Mike Diolosa (Qloo CTO) is judging the depth of Qloo use.
- **For Cedric:** keep it concrete and human - "the right partner, and the pitch to win them." No jargon.
- **For Jason:** the business model line is for him. He picks the extra $25k and he's a podcaster.
- **Fallback if the demo stalls:** "While that loads - the key move is the reverse check, their listeners liking me back, which is what makes a swap actually convert." Then continue.

## One-liner (if cut to 15 seconds)

"Swapcast uses Qloo's taste graph to find podcasts your size whose listeners like yours - and checks that yours like them back - then drafts the promo-swap pitch with the evidence. It turns podcast growth from a guess into a match."
